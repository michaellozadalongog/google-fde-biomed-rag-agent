# Acoustic Output and Thermal/Mechanical Safety Indices (Synthetic Primer)

## Indices
The Mechanical Index (MI) estimates the likelihood of cavitation and equals peak rarefactional pressure (MPa, derated) divided by the square root of center frequency (MHz). The Thermal Index (TI) estimates temperature rise; variants are TIS (soft tissue), TIB (bone), and TIC (cranial).

## Limits
Regulatory track 3 limits cap MI at 1.9 and derated spatial-peak temporal-average intensity (ISPTA.3) at 720 mW/cm2. For obstetric scanning, the ALARA principle applies and TI should generally be kept below 0.7; for fetal scanning above TI 1.0, exposure time should be limited.

## Practice
Use the lowest output and shortest dwell time that yields a diagnostic image (ALARA). Doppler modes typically have higher TI than B-mode.
