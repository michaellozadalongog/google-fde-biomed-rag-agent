# Agar-Gelatin Phantom Fabrication Notes (Synthetic Lab SOP)

## Materials
Agar 2-3% w/v provides structural stability. Gelatin 5-8% w/v adjusts stiffness. Cellulose or graphite powder (0.5-2% w/v) acts as acoustic scatterers producing speckle. Glycerol (5-10% v/v) raises the speed of sound toward 1540 m/s. Benzalkonium chloride or a similar preservative prevents microbial growth.

## Procedure
Heat deionized water to 90 °C, dissolve agar while stirring, cool to 60 °C, then add gelatin and glycerol. Add scatterers last and stir slowly to avoid air bubbles. Degas under vacuum for 10 minutes; trapped bubbles cause bright artifacts and shadowing. Pour into the mold and refrigerate at 4 °C for 12 hours.

## Inclusions
Stiffer inclusions for elastography use higher agar concentration (4%). Anechoic cyst targets can be produced with water-filled balloons or by leaving voids with removable rods.

## Storage and shelf life
Store sealed at 4 °C. Agar phantoms dehydrate over time; measure mass weekly and discard when mass loss exceeds 5%, because dehydration changes speed of sound and attenuation. Typical shelf life is 4-6 weeks.
