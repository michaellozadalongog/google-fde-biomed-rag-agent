# Ultrasound Probe Reprocessing Guidance (Synthetic Summary)

## Spaulding classification
Probes contacting intact skin are non-critical and require low-level disinfection. Endocavitary probes contact mucous membranes, are semi-critical, and require high-level disinfection (HLD) even when a probe cover is used, because covers can have perforations.

## Workflow
1. Pre-clean at point of use: wipe gel off immediately.
2. Inspect the lens and cable for cracks; a cracked lens fails inspection and the probe must be removed from service because fluid ingress can create electrical leakage risk.
3. Perform HLD using an approved method (e.g., automated hydrogen peroxide mist or validated chemical soak), following the manufacturer contact time.
4. Rinse, dry, and store in a clean, ventilated cabinet.
5. Log the probe ID, cycle ID, and operator for traceability.

## Common failures
Using alcohol wipes on the lens can degrade the acoustic lens material. Exceeding soak time can damage seals.
